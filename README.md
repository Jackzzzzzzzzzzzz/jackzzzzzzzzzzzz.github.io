## About

I study how intestinal epithelial cells maintain tissue function and regulate mucosal immunity. As a PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, I combine molecular and cellular biology with bioinformatics and machine learning to investigate these processes. My broader interests include multi-omics approaches to understanding mechanisms of cancer and chronic inflammatory disease.

## Current Research

**RNA processing and intestinal immunity.** I investigate how the RNA-binding protein hnRNP I (PTBP1) supports epithelial function and how its loss alters gene regulation, antimicrobial defense, and inflammation in the colon.

**Cell states and tissue organization.** I integrate single-cell RNA sequencing and spatial transcriptomics with molecular assays and quantitative imaging to examine epithelial specialization, immune-cell organization, and their relationships within intact tissue.

## Selected Projects

### Spatial mapping of intestinal epithelial organization

*Ongoing research*

I combine spatial transcriptomics with single-cell reference profiles to investigate how epithelial hnRNP I loss changes colonic organization. My analysis includes tissue annotation, computational unrolling along proximal–distal and serosa–luminal axes, cell-state mapping, and regional gene-expression analysis. In this mouse model, spatial maps show altered epithelial domain organization and distributions of progenitor-associated signals, connecting transcriptional cell states with their anatomical context.
